    Production · Ontario Public Service

    EWRS Employee Workspace Reservation System

    The desk-booking platform for Ontario government employees. I took a codebase stuck on Angular 12 and brought it to Angular 21 and .NET 10, while shipping new features the whole way.

    • ~400commits
    • 65+branches
    • 9majors
    • 0regressions
    The problem
    A production Angular 12 frontend and .NET Azure Functions backend accumulating unsupported tooling, untyped forms and deprecated Material APIs.
    What I owned
    Both repos, frontend and backend: the migration, the design system, and new features end to end.
    The hard part
    Nine major versions, including the Material MDC theming migration and strongly typed reactive forms, without freezing feature work.
    The result
    Angular 21 and .NET 10 on isolated Azure Functions v4, a system-aware dark mode on shared design tokens, and zero feature regressions.

    Production · Ontario Public Service

    CALM Calendar And Leave Management

    Leave requests, approvals and a shared team calendar. Approvals and onboarding run through email links, so I treated every link as untrusted input.

    • 187commits
    • 16Next.js
    • 19React
    • 7Prisma
    The problem
    Approvals over email and invitations, with employees, managers and admins all needing different, enforceable access.
    What I owned
    Onboarding, auth hardening, scheduled jobs, transactional email, observability and developer docs.
    The hard part
    Making single-use links safe under concurrency: hashed tokens, conditional updates and atomic membership changes so two clicks can't both win.
    The result
    Fail-closed RBAC, token-validated approve and deny links, automated reminders and expiry, App Insights tracing, and Vitest coverage across services and routes.

    Interactive · invitation lifecycle

    state: issued
